Membership is open to all architecture students and urban & regional planning students at the Technical University of Kenya. Fill in the form and the committee will email you a welcome with what happens next.

  • Peer reviews and studio support
  • One association for both architecture and urban & regional planning
  • Workshops, talks and site visits
  • First word on events and exhibitions

We only use your details to run the association and contact you about ASA TUK. Questions? See the FAQ.
