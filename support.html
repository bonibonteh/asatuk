Support.

Your support helps turn ideas into opportunities.

Individuals, alumni, professionals and organisations can all support ASA TUK. Contributions help us run programmes that give architecture and urban & regional planning students practical experience, mentorship and a place to show their work.

Support ASA TUKPartner with us instead

Why it matters

Help us create meaningful opportunities for architecture students through education, mentorship, professional engagement, innovation and community-based initiatives.

Where your support goes.

Contributions and sponsorship can support these areas of student life.

  • Student exhibitions
  • Architecture competitions
  • Workshops
  • Professional mentorship
  • Site visits
  • Learning resources
  • Community design projects
  • Student innovation
  • Digital resources
  • Student development programmes
