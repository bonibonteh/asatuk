Questions

FAQ.

Who can join ASA TUK?

Any architecture student or urban & regional planning student at the Technical University of Kenya. Use the registration form.

How much does it cost?

Membership details are confirmed by the committee. Ask through the contact page and we will reply.

What happens after I register?

The committee receives your details by email and will get in touch with a welcome and details of upcoming events.

How do I find out about events?

Check the events page and news, and register so you are told first.

Can I share my project?

Yes. Send a short description and images through the contact page and we will consider it for the archive.

How can my firm or organisation sponsor or collaborate?

Choose the Sponsor or Collaborate topic on the contact page.

Are urban & regional planning students part of ASA TUK?

Yes. ASA TUK has two chapters, each with its own council: the Architecture Chapter Council and the Urban & Regional Planning Chapter Council. See the leadership page.

How do I run for the committee?

Committee roles are filled by the association. Ask the committee for the current process.
