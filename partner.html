Partner.

ASA TUK welcomes collaboration with organisations and individuals who support architecture and the built environment.

We are the student association for architecture and urban & regional planning students at the Technical University of Kenya. Partnerships help us offer mentorship, site visits, competitions and exhibitions that go beyond the lecture room.

Why partner with ASA TUK?

  1. Connect with emerging architecture talentMeet students of architecture and urban & regional planning at the Technical University of Kenya.
  2. Support professional developmentMentorship, talks and workshops help students prepare for practice.
  3. Increase visibility among studentsReach students through our events, news and student community.
  4. Take part in industry and academic engagementJoin site visits, public lectures and student exhibitions.
  5. Support innovation and designBack competitions, hackathons and design-build projects.
  6. Contribute to student developmentScholarships, resources and equipment make a practical difference.
  7. Support community-focused initiativesHelp students apply design to real community needs.
